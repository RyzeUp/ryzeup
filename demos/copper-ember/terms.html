Terms

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Bookings

  • A booking request is confirmed once we've replied to you by email or phone.
  • We hold tables for 15 minutes. If you're running late, please call us.
  • Tables are usually yours for two hours, or two and a half hours for groups of 6 or more.
  • For groups of more than 8, please call us to book.

Changes and cancellations

If your plans change, let us know as early as you can by phone or email so we can offer the table to someone else.

Allergies

Please tell us about any allergies when you book and again when you arrive. Our kitchen handles all 14 major allergens, so we can't guarantee any dish is completely free from traces.

Prices and menu

The menu on this website is a sample and changes with the seasons. Prices include VAT. A discretionary 10% service charge is added for tables of 6 or more.

Using this website

We try to keep the information here accurate, but opening hours and dishes can change at short notice. The content and design of this site belong to Copper & Ember and may not be copied without permission.
