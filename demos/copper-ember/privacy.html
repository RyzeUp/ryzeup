Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how Copper & Ember ("we", "us") collects and uses your personal information when you use this website. We follow the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who we are

Copper & Ember, 1 Example Street, Burton upon Trent, DE14 0XX. You can contact us about your data at hello@example.com.

What we collect

When you book a table we collect:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • the date, time, number of guests and table you chose
  • anything you tell us in the notes box, such as allergies or a special occasion

Allergy information can be health information. We only use it to prepare your food safely, and only the staff serving you see it.

Why we use it

  • To manage your booking and contact you about it. The legal basis is taking steps to enter a contract with you.
  • To keep you safe if you tell us about allergies. The legal basis is your explicit consent, which you give by telling us.

We do not send marketing emails unless you separately ask to receive them, and we never sell your details.

Who we share it with

Booking forms are delivered to us through Formspree, a form-handling service. The website is hosted by Vercel. Both act on our instructions and keep data secure. Some of their servers are outside the UK, protected by approved safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep booking details for up to 12 months after your visit, then delete them.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies, and its fonts are hosted on the site itself.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email us and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
