One fire, one pass, no shortcuts

The whole menu runs off a single wood-fired grill and oven. Meat rests over the embers, vegetables char in the flames and puddings bake as the fire dies down at the end of the night.

We buy from growers and butchers around Staffordshire and pour beer brewed a few streets away. If it isn't good that week, it isn't on.
