Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Greg Szadkowski, trading as refurbishment24 ("we", "us"). We fit kitchens and bathrooms, and carry out tiling and wet room work, across Derbyshire. For anything about your personal data, contact us at hello@refurbishment24.co.uk or on 07700 900000.

What we collect

  • When you use the quote form: your name, phone number, town or village, email address (if given), the type of job and any details you add.
  • When you call, text, WhatsApp or email us: your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us, including any photos of your room.
  • If we go on to quote or do the work: your address and the details needed to carry out and invoice the job.

We don't use analytics or advertising cookies, and this website doesn't set any cookies.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you a quote: because you've asked us to, before entering a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To carry out, invoice and guarantee the work: to perform our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To keep business and tax records: to meet our legal obligations (Article 6(1)(c)).

We never sell your details or use them for marketing lists.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree, if you send the form by email. Formspree delivers the message to us and may process it in the United States under appropriate safeguards.
  • WhatsApp (Meta), if you choose to message us on WhatsApp. Your message is handled under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
  • OpenStreetMap and cdnjs (Cloudflare), which supply the map on the home page; your browser connects to their servers to load it.
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces on this site; your browser connects to Google's servers to load them.
  • Our email and phone providers, and our accountant where needed for tax.

How long we keep it

If an enquiry doesn't lead to any work, we delete it within 12 months. If we do work for you, we keep job and invoice records for 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as required by HMRC.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, delete it or restrict how we use it, and object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
