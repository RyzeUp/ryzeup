What Greg fits

Everything from the strip-out to the last bead of silicone, done by the same person who quoted for it.

Kitchen refits

Your new kitchen fitted from bare walls up: old units out, cabinets set level and square, worktops cut and joined, appliances and sink in.

  • Strip-out and disposal of the old kitchen
  • Units, worktops, upstands and end panels
  • Splashback and floor tiling

Bathroom refits

Complete bathrooms and en-suites: suite, shower, vanity, tiling and finishing, with the room left clean and ready to use.

  • Baths, showers, toilets and vanity units
  • Boarding and preparing walls for tile
  • En-suites and cloakrooms

Tiling

Walls and floors in ceramic, porcelain and large-format tiles, set out so the cuts land where you won't notice them.

  • Kitchens, bathrooms, hallways and utility rooms
  • Floor levelling before tiling
  • Re-grouting and repairs

Wet rooms

Level-access showers and full wet rooms, waterproofed beneath the tiles and laid to fall so the water goes where it should.

  • Tanking and waterproofing
  • Sloped floors and linear drains
  • Walk-in showers and screens

Fitting only. Greg doesn't sell kitchens or bathrooms. Buy yours wherever suits you (a showroom, a DIY store, online) and he'll fit it.

How a job runs

One person from first look to final clean, so nothing gets lost between trades.

  1. 1

    Look and quote

    Greg comes out, measures up and talks through what you've chosen. You get a written price.

  2. 2

    Strip-out

    The old kitchen or bathroom comes out and the room is taken back to a sound base.

  3. 3

    Prepare

    Pipework moved, walls boarded, floors levelled and wet areas waterproofed.

  4. 4

    Fit and tile

    Units or suite fitted, then walls and floors tiled to a careful set-out.

  5. 5

    Finish

    Grout, silicone, trims and fittings, then a proper clean before handover.
