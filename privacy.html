Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Who I am

Ryze Up is run by David Lopez, a sole trader based in Derbyshire. I am the data controller for personal information collected through this website. You can contact me at david@ryzeup.co.uk.

What I collect

  • Details you send through the contact form: your name, business name, phone number or email address, and your message.
  • Details you give me when you call, message or email me.
  • Basic technical information your browser sends when you visit, such as IP address and browser type, which my hosting provider may log for security.

Why I use it

I use your details to reply to your enquiry, to provide a quote, and to deliver and invoice any work you ask me to do. The legal bases are your request for a quote or service (steps before a contract, and the contract itself) and my legitimate interest in running a small business and replying to messages.

I do not sell your information, and I do not send marketing emails unless you ask me to.

Who I share it with

  • Formspree, which processes contact form submissions and forwards them to me by email.
  • Vercel, which hosts this website.
  • Accounting, invoicing and payment providers, only if you become a client.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use safeguards recognised under UK data protection law.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for six years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as HMRC requires.

Cookies

This website does not set cookies for tracking or advertising. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to object to how I use it. Email me and I will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
