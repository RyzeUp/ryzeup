Common questions

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, just ask.

D David at Ryze UpTap a question to see my answer
Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. You tell me about your business and send me your photos and details. I handle the design, building, hosting setup and launch.

What's the difference between the Starter Site and the Full Site?

The Starter Site is one page with everything a customer needs: what you do, where you are, and how to get in touch. The Full Site has up to 5 pages, room for galleries or before and after photos, and I set up your Google Business Profile for you.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. The website is a one-off price. The Care Plan and Local SEO Plan are optional, and you can cancel any month.

Who owns the domain name?

If you buy the domain yourself, it's registered in your name and stays yours whatever happens. If you'd rather not deal with it, I can find a good domain for your business and set it up for you.

Can I change things on my website myself?

Your site is hand-built rather than made on a drag-and-drop builder, so changes go through me. Small text and photo changes each month are included in the Care Plan, or I can quote for one-off changes.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. I can redesign it or rebuild it from scratch, keeping your web address. Book a free chat and I'll take a look at what you have now.

What do you need from me?

Your logo if you have one, a few good photos of your business or work, your services and prices, opening hours and contact details. I can help write the wording.

How do I pay?

Once you've seen your mockup and we've talked it through, you pay a deposit of half the website price to start the build. You can pay in cash in person, or online by card through Stripe.
