About
Vérité.

Founded in 2018, Vérité Studio sits in the heart of Manchester's Northern Quarter. We built the studio around one principle: your cut should work every morning, not just when you leave the chair.

We specialise in precision cuts, lived-in colour, and sharp grooming. No upselling, no fuss — just consistent, honest results from a team that knows its craft.
